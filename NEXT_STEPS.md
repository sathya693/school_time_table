You asked why the schedule is not full even though there are free teachers and free time slots. That is the perfect question to ask, and the "Summary" page you were looking at is designed to help you answer exactly that.

### The Missing Piece: Defining Courses

The "missing piece" is the **Courses**.

The scheduler can only place lessons that have been defined in the system. While you have available teachers and the sections have available time slots, the system has not been told **what to teach** in those empty slots.

The "Section Fill Analysis" table correctly shows that each section is "Under Scheduled" by 10 periods. The "Teacher Workload" table correctly shows that the teachers have free time. The application is now waiting for you, the administrator, to create the courses to fill those 10 empty periods for each section.

### Action Required: Your Next Step

To fill the schedule and resolve the "Under Scheduled" status, you need to:

1.  Go back to **Step 5: Teacher Assignments** in the Setup Wizard.
2.  For each section that is under-scheduled, you must **add more courses** until its "Assigned Periods" total equals its "Available Periods" (e.g., 40/40).

**Example:**
*   The summary shows "Grade 6 - Section A" has **30/40** periods assigned.
*   You need to create **10 more periods** worth of courses for this section.
*   You could click "Add Assignment" for an available teacher (like Ms. Patel who has 30 free periods), select "Grade 6 - Section A", choose a subject like "Art", and assign **5** periods.
*   Then, you could add another assignment for "Grade 6 - Section A" for "History" for **5** periods with another available teacher.
*   After saving, the summary for "Grade 6 - Section A" would update to **40/40**, and the teachers' workloads would update as well.

Once all sections are fully scheduled in Step 5, the "Generate Timetable" button on the Dashboard will produce a complete, conflict-free schedule with no gaps.

The application is working correctly by showing you this data gap. You have all the tools you need to fix it by defining the complete curriculum in Step 5!
